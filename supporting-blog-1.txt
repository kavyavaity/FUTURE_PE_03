# What Causes Frizzy Hair?

Frizzy hair happens when the outer layer of the hair cuticle becomes dry and raised, allowing moisture from the air to enter the hair strands. This creates puffiness, rough texture, and unmanageable hair.

Several factors contribute to frizzy hair, especially in urban environments like Mumbai.

## Humidity & Mumbai Weather

Mumbai’s humid climate is one of the biggest reasons behind frizzy hair. Excess moisture in the air causes hair strands to swell, making them appear rough and puffy.

Women with dry, curly, chemically treated, or damaged hair are especially affected during monsoon and summer seasons.

---

## Heat Styling Damage

Frequent use of:

* Hair straighteners
* Curling irons
* Blow dryers

can weaken hair proteins and remove natural moisture from the hair shaft, leading to dryness and frizz.

---

## Chemical Treatments

Over-processing from:

* Hair coloring
* Bleaching
* Rebonding
* Smoothening

can damage the protective hair layer and make hair more prone to breakage and roughness.

---

## Pollution & Hard Water

Daily exposure to pollution, dust, sweat, and hard water can strip natural oils from the scalp and leave hair dehydrated.

In cities like Mumbai, environmental stress often makes hair look dull and lifeless over time.

Living in Mumbai means constantly dealing with weather conditions that affect hair health.

## Humidity Weakens Hair Texture
