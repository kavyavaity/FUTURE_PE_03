# 1. What Is a Content Cluster Strategy?

A content cluster is an SEO structure where:

* One **main pillar page** covers a broad topic
* Multiple **supporting blogs** target related subtopics
* All pages internally link to one another

This structure helps Google understand:

* Your website expertise
* Topic relevance
* Keyword relationships
* Local authority within a niche

For GlowUp Beauty Salon, the main SEO focus is:

* Haircare
* Bridal beauty
* Self-care
* Premium salon services in Mumbai
