## Bridal Beauty Preparation Tips

### Start Skincare Early

Healthy skin creates the perfect makeup base. Start facials and skincare treatments at least 2–3 months before your wedding.

### Follow a Haircare Routine

Regular trims, hair spas, and nourishment treatments improve hair texture before bridal hairstyling sessions.

### Schedule Trial Makeup Sessions

A bridal makeup trial helps finalize your desired look and reduces wedding-day stress.

### Prioritize Self-Care

Wedding planning can become overwhelming. Relaxation treatments, spa sessions, and skincare appointments help maintain emotional and physical wellness.
